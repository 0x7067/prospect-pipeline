# Pousada Betânia — Kimi Variant

Independent static redesign concept for Pousada Betânia, built inside the injected variant directory.

## What this is

A production-grade static site concept that reorganizes the pousada’s public offer — hospitality, events and tranquility — into three clear paths, with direct booking and contact always visible. It is not the official site and has not been published or approved by the business.

## Files

- `index.html` — homepage with hero, three paths, hosting, events, services, gallery, location and contact.
- `proposal.html` — evidence-led explanation of the independent proposal, sources and design decisions.
- `rationale.html` — redirect-only page to `proposal.html`.
- `styles.css` — responsive, accessible design system based on the documented brand colors and typography.
- `script.js` — mobile navigation, header scroll behavior and smooth anchor scrolling.
- `assets/` — local copies of the logo and official photos sourced from the original site.
- `README.md` — this file.
- `SITE_REVIEW.md` — desktop and mobile validation notes.
- `SOURCE_MANIFEST.md` — provenance of every sourced claim and asset.

## How to view

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step is required.

## Evidence boundary

All business-specific claims come from the supplied prospect, brand source, product and design briefs, plus the official site evidence captured on 2026-07-17. No invented claims, segments, testimonials or metrics were used.
