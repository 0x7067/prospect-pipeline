# Design System: Evidence-bounded redesign seed

<!-- Seed scaffold: Impeccable craft must replace inferred choices with an implemented, evidence-compatible system. -->

## Overview

Creative North Star: earn trust through category-specific clarity, not generic AI decoration. This is a brand/marketing concept; it must never imply endorsement.

## Colors

Preserve only colors that can be defensibly inferred from supplied evidence or original-site captures. Otherwise generate a restrained accessible palette and record the rationale.

## Typography

Choose a distinctive, readable hierarchy suitable for the documented category. Body copy must remain WCAG AA and comfortable on mobile.

## Elevation

Flat by default. Use elevation only to communicate interaction or hierarchy; avoid nested cards and decorative glass effects.

## Components

Use semantic HTML, visible focus, touch-sized controls, inert demo actions, and a separate rationale page. Establish information hierarchy before decoration.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do tailor the system to the verified rebuild opportunity. Don't copy proprietary logos/photos/layouts/substantial text, invent claims, fabricate proof, or use a generic AI landing-page template.
