# Final selection decision

**Selected winner:** Kimi V2 (`iterations/kimi/v2`)

**Decision date:** 2026-07-18

## Decision

Kimi V2 is promoted as the single canonical production candidate. The AFK primary build remains preserved as a historical comparison artifact under `iterations/afk-primary/`; it is not a second live candidate.

## Why Kimi V2 won

- Stronger architecture-specific hero treatment on desktop and mobile.
- Clearer visual proof through the supplied residential imagery.
- Stronger mobile conversion path: visible project CTA, compact navigation, and complete mobile journey.
- Passed the independent style gate with an average score of **4.17/5**, with no dimension below the required threshold.
- Browser validation passed for index and proposal pages at desktop and mobile sizes: no console errors, page errors, request failures, or horizontal overflow; mobile menu open/close behavior was verified.

## Trade-off acknowledged

The AFK primary build has excellent editorial hierarchy and strong brand-safe copy, but its hero imagery is less compelling and its mobile conversion clarity is weaker. It is retained only for provenance and future reference.

## Scope

This is a candidate selection, not publication authorization. Existing approval/publication receipts remain build-bound and must not be reused for the promoted Kimi V2 files. A fresh current approval is required before any external publication or outreach.
