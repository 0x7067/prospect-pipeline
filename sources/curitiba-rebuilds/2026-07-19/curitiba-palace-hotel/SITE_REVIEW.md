# Site review

## Design thesis

**A considered arrival in Curitiba.** The homepage moves from welcome to room confidence, breakfast, practical services, central location, and direct reservation. Deep pine, restrained gold, editorial display type, and a consistent photographic treatment give the hotel a quiet, traditional hospitality voice without inventing brand assets.

## Art-directed moments

1. The full-bleed arrival hero pairs the central-Curitiba promise with a direct availability panel.
2. The room section uses an asymmetric image pair to make comfort tangible before the tariff action.
3. The dark breakfast and location sections create deliberate pauses in the page rhythm.
4. The final reservation panel closes the journey with a clear official-channel action.

## Heuristic tests

- **Logo-removal:** the address, Curitiba context, verified amenities, and arrival sequence still identify the hotel concept without the wordmark. Pass.
- **Competitor-swap:** replacing the name would leave the supplied address, 30+ years, services, breakfast, and direct-rate promise structurally embedded. Pass.
- **Squint:** the eye moves from arrival promise to booking action, room proof, breakfast, services, location, and final reservation. Pass.
- **Five-second:** central Curitiba, hotel identity, and the direct reservation action are visible immediately. Pass.
- **Below-fold:** each section advances a distinct lodging decision without social-feed filler or repeated card grids. Pass.
- **Mobile-native:** navigation collapses, the booking panel stacks, image pairs remain readable, and actions stay thumb-sized. Pass.

## Accessibility and integrity

Semantic landmarks, visible labels, keyboard-operable navigation, reduced-motion handling, responsive layouts, descriptive image alternatives, and high-contrast surfaces are included. No unsupported rates, room categories, testimonials, awards, or operational claims were added. Stock photography is disclosed as directional imagery in the proposal.

## Delivery status

Early visual direction only. It is not production-complete and is not ready for publication. Business validation, owned photography, confirmed identity assets, reservations integration, content approval, and a full production accessibility review remain outside this bounded deliverable.
