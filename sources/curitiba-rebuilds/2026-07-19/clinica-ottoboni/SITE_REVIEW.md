# Site review

## Status

**Early visual direction only.** Not production-complete, publication-ready, approved, or human-approved. Review is a builder self-audit, not independent approval.

## Thesis and art direction

This concept can only belong to Clínica Ottoboni because its three documented disciplines become one continuous consultation atlas: specialty selection, integrated handoff, then direct contact. Three distinct moments carry that route: the hero compass, the interactive specialty crossing, and the typographic braid/contact destination. The image rule excludes unsupported clinical imagery in favor of original atlas geometry. Color evolves from white orientation to pale-blue selection, blue integration, and deep-ink destination; plum marks handoffs only.

## Anti-template tests

- **Logo removal:** Pass as a design-direction test. The three-specialty route, compass, crossing and braid retain the documented integrated-care idea without the wordmark; formal brand recognition still depends on future identity confirmation.
- **Competitor swap:** Pass as a structure test. A name swap would leave a route explicitly built around Ottoboni’s documented trio and integrated-care positioning; another clinic with different evidence would require different stops and route logic.
- **Squint:** Pass. The page reads as four intentionally changing fields: open white compass, pale crossing, saturated braid, dark destination.
- **Five-second:** Pass by static inspection. The first view states the clinic name, all three specialties and “um cuidado integrado,” with a clear route into specialty choice.
- **Below-fold:** Pass. The interactive crossing and full-width braid are separately composed rather than generic card rows.
- **Mobile-native:** Pass by responsive-code inspection. Navigation has an explicit menu state, the specialty crossing becomes a horizontal snap route, atlas geometry crops edge-to-edge, and phones precede the address. This is not a substitute for device testing before production.

## Accessibility and behavior checks

- Semantic landmarks, skip link, visible focus, keyboard-operable tabs and reduced-motion handling are present.
- Menu communicates `aria-expanded` and supports Escape; tab/panel relationships use ARIA plus arrow, Home and End navigation.
- Contact controls and mobile menu meet the 44px target.
- Homepage contains no proposal link or concept/disclosure language.
- No form, analytics, external script, font request, publication action or prospect contact action was added.

## Remaining dependencies

Production would require business review of content, identity/color confirmation, clinician and credential content decisions, rights-cleared media, browser/device and assistive-technology testing, privacy/legal review, analytics decisions, and implementation of the preferred appointment workflow.
