# Site review

## Design thesis

**Station timetable meets hotel key sleeve.** The homepage turns the central-location proposition into wayfinding: compact route labels, a key-sleeve hero object, a diagrammatic utility plan, a code-native street network, and a large event-capacity seal. Ink-violet carries the sense of navigation; electric coral marks action and route; cool white evolves into near-black as the journey moves from arrival to conversion.

Display type uses Georgia for a warm editorial/hospitality voice; Trebuchet MS is the deliberately neutral wayfinding body face. Imagery follows one rule: architectural and navigational information is rendered as flat, code-native linework with one coral route or annotation, never as invented photography.

## Art-directed moments

1. The rotated key sleeve makes address and essential amenities the hero proof object.
2. The room utility plan spatializes verified amenities instead of repeating cards.
3. The dark street-network panel makes verified proximity and address the visual narrative.
4. The circular “300” event seal gives the strongest B2B fact singular emphasis.

## Heuristic tests

- **Logo-removal:** without the wordmark, Curitiba-centre wayfinding, key sleeve, address, room utility plan and event-capacity seal still identify this as a practical central hotel/event concept.
- **Competitor-swap:** replacing the name alone would leave mismatched evidence: the supplied address, nearby Curitiba places, verified amenity set and 300-person capacity are structurally embedded. Pass.
- **Squint:** the eye moves from oversized location promise to coral action, then two routes, pale accommodation proof, dark map, event seal and dark close. Hierarchy remains legible. Pass.
- **Five-second:** “central Curitiba hotel,” reservation action, and stay/event split are visible immediately. Pass.
- **Below-fold:** each section advances a distinct decision—room utility, access, event scale, action—without tourism filler or repeated card grids. Pass.
- **Mobile-native:** navigation collapses, the hero reads before its proof object, route choices stack as rows, the map precedes address copy, and all actions remain full-content and thumb-readable. Pass.

## Accessibility and integrity

Semantic landmarks, a skip link, visible text labels, high-contrast surfaces, reduced-motion handling, and a keyboard-operable menu are included. No form, fake availability, rate, room category, telephone, email, testimonial, award, or unsupported operating detail appears.
