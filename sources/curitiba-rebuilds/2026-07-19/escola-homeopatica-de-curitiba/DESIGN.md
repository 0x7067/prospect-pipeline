# Design direction: A casa viva da formação

## Compositional thesis

The page behaves like a calm institutional house rather than a sales funnel. A vertical cobalt “door” anchors the opening; the course is organized as an editorial decision path; the social outpatient clinic changes the page field from paper-white to deep blue. Legacy, study and service appear as connected rooms, with contact presented once as a clear threshold.

## System

- **Palette:** blue-neutral paper `#eef2f8`, white `#fff`, ink `#12213d`, institutional cobalt `#153f98`, deep cobalt `#0b255e`, and a restrained warm line `#c7a86a`. The cool paper belongs to the documented blue/white equity; exact colors are a concept palette pending production brand sampling.
- **Typography:** Georgia is the display face for scholarly, human editorial character; an Optima/Candara humanist stack is the body/UI face for approachable clarity. Both are local system-safe and make no external request.
- **Scale:** large display type uses `clamp()`; body measure remains near 65 characters. Hairline rules and numbered labels carry hierarchy without card chrome.
- **Image treatment:** no unverified photography is used. Abstract architectural linework is CSS-only and always monochrome, cropped by a hard rectangular frame, never decorative gradients or glass.
- **Color evolution:** paper opening → white course decision path → deep cobalt social mission → paper institutional/contact close.
- **Motion:** restrained reveal transitions only when motion is allowed; content remains visible without JavaScript.

## Art-directed moments

1. **The open door:** asymmetric masthead with a tall cobalt threshold, oversized course statement, and a compact AMHB cue.
2. **The study table:** professional audiences and a vertical course inquiry rail arranged as an editorial spread, not repeated cards.
3. **The clinic night field:** full-bleed cobalt chapter with an architectural crosshair and oversized “social” word fragment.
4. **The address threshold:** contact information becomes a typographic place composition, with the street number used as spatial punctuation.

## Mobile-native rhythm

Navigation collapses to an explicit menu; editorial spreads become linear reading sequences; large type scales fluidly; buttons become full-width where useful; touch targets are at least 44px. The mobile order is proposition → audience → evidence cue → action.

## Content and status

All business facts are bounded to `prospect.json`, `PRODUCT.md`, and `BRAND_SOURCE.md`. No intake dates, tuition, curriculum details, outcomes, staff roster, hours, or response promises are invented. This is an **early visual direction**, not production-complete or ready for publication.
