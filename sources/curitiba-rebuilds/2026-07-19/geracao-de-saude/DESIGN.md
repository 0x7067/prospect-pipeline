# Design System: Care itinerary

## Creative north star

Turn the homepage into a quiet care navigator: the family's present situation is the organizing structure, and every path resolves to one free-assessment invitation. The page should feel like a calm, well-prepared conversation rather than a catalogue of services.

Physical scene: a family member reads the site on a phone in a softly lit waiting room or at a kitchen table, needing clarity without urgency theatre.

## Composition

- **Arrival / orientation:** an asymmetric split between one decisive promise and one verified caregiver photograph, with a short orientation note rather than statistics.
- **Situation ledger:** residence, hospital or consultation, post-operative support, and urgent coverage appear as a scan-friendly decision sequence, not repeated cards.
- **Human continuity:** two offset photographic crops frame a central statement about respecting routine; service contexts become a single typographic line.
- **Resolution:** a deep-green assessment field closes the journey with one primary link, the verified phone number, and the Curitiba address without implying Curitiba-only coverage.

## Color

Committed strategy based on verified brand rendering:

- Deep green `#23382B` / `#2D4337`: identity, high-confidence close, navigation ink.
- Warm gold `#CE9F43`: sparse decision emphasis and interaction cues, never decorative gradients.
- Neutral `#DCD7D1`: photographic interlude and quiet structural separation.
- True cool off-white `#F3F4F0`: readable base without generic cream styling.

Color evolves from light orientation, to white decision space, to warm-neutral human context, to deep-green resolution.

## Typography

Voice words: composed, humane, attentive. Use **Bodoni Moda** for the expressive display voice and **Source Sans 3** for readable navigation, body copy, and controls. This pairing contrasts sculptural warmth with practical clarity without using the register's reflex fonts. Display tracking never exceeds `-0.035em`; body copy remains at least 16px with comfortable measure.

## Image treatment

Use only verified first-party caregiver/service photographs. Crop decisively around gestures and proximity, reduce saturation slightly, keep contrast soft, and use square or tall rectangular frames with no decorative rounding. Never use imagery as unsupported proof; captions describe care context only.

## Interaction and motion

- One persistent assessment path in header and closing section.
- Situation rows have restrained directional movement and clear focus states.
- Content is visible by default; progressive reveal is an enhancement only.
- Reduced-motion mode removes transforms and smooth scrolling.
- Touch targets are at least 44px and the mobile navigation is keyboard-operable.

## Mobile-native rhythm

The hero becomes promise → next step → photograph. The situation ledger collapses into compact question/answer blocks, imagery becomes a two-column diptych after the core statement, and the closing assessment actions stack full width. Section spacing tightens selectively, preserving generous pauses around decisions.

## Evidence and conversion rules

- No metrics, testimonials, years, team size, or coverage claims beyond supplied evidence.
- Service language is limited to documented contexts.
- No form is simulated; the assessment CTA routes to the verified first-party contact page and urgent call action uses the verified phone number.
- The public homepage contains no redesign, proposal, prototype, or disclosure language and never links to the proposal.

## Do / don't

Do use asymmetry, editorial-scale type without adopting an editorial-magazine scaffold, decisive photography, and one navigational sequence. Don't use repeated card grids, glass effects, gradients, decorative counters, tiny eyebrow labels on every section, or invented reassurance.
