# Site review

## Design thesis

The homepage is a quiet care map: a family enters through its immediate situation and follows one continuous route to an assessment. Three art-directed moments carry the idea: the asymmetrical split-path opening, the numbered situation-to-care route, and the dark, focused assessment invitation.

The display/body pair is Bodoni Moda and Source Sans 3. Photography follows one rule: first-party caregiver imagery, desaturated slightly and held in calm, decisive rectangular crops around presence and proximity. Color evolves from cool off-white to white clarity, then the warm neutral care story, and finally deep green at conversion. On mobile, content becomes a deliberate single-column sequence with paired documentary crops rather than a compressed desktop grid.

## Required tests

- **Logo-removal test:** removing the wordmark still leaves the care-map route, caregiver crops, green/gold palette, and family-centered language. The composition remains category- and prospect-connected.
- **Competitor-swap test:** a generic care provider could not be swapped in without changing the verified situation list, assessment language, contact details, visual assets, and specific brand palette. The design is anchored to supplied Geração de Saúde evidence.
- **Squint test:** the hierarchy reads as one large opening promise, a strongly ordered middle route, an editorial human-care pause, and one dark conversion destination. No repeated card field competes for attention.
- **Five-second test:** “cuidado especializado para idosos,” “mais tranquilidade para sua família,” and the route to find the right care are visible immediately.
- **Below-fold test:** every later section advances the decision: choose a situation, understand contexts of presence, or start the verified free assessment. There is no decorative filler or unsupported proof block.
- **Mobile-native test:** navigation collapses accessibly; the hero separates promise, orientation, and image; each route step becomes question → answer → action; controls retain large touch areas; typography and spacing are independently tuned for narrow screens.

## Accessibility and behavior

Semantic landmarks, a skip link, explicit image alternatives, keyboard-operable navigation, descriptive action labels, visible underlines, reduced-motion handling, and high-contrast foregrounds are included. There are no dead form controls. JavaScript only manages the mobile menu, current year, and progressive reveal; core navigation works without it.

## Evidence boundary

The build intentionally omits zero counters, testimonials, response-time claims, certifications, and unverified service metrics. Curitiba is presented as a contact address, not exclusive coverage.
