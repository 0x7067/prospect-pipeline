# Design System: Evidence-bounded redesign seed

<!-- Seed scaffold: Impeccable craft must replace inferred choices with an implemented, evidence-compatible system. -->

## Overview

Creative North Star: Make the school itself the interface: a calm, evidence-led journey that lets education leaders move from a learning-space problem to the right architectural intervention and a practical first conversation.

Compositional thesis: treat the page as a project line moving through a school. The line connects four art-directed moments: a project-notebook hero, question-to-intervention stations, a full-width technical principle, and a dark-green conversation room. The page should feel like moving through a considered architectural briefing, not browsing a service catalog.

## Colors

Preserve only colors that can be defensibly inferred from supplied evidence or original-site captures. Otherwise generate a restrained accessible palette and record the rationale.

Implemented direction: a near-neutral field subtly tinted toward the primary green, progressing from light planning surfaces to a deep technical green close. A controlled lime mark is reserved for route nodes and decisive annotations. No decorative gradients.

## Typography

Choose a distinctive, readable hierarchy suitable for the documented category. Body copy must remain WCAG AA and comfortable on mobile.

Implemented pairing: Spectral for calm, architectural display forms and Archivo for precise body/UI copy. Display spacing stays above the -0.04em floor; body measures are capped for reading comfort.

## Elevation

Flat by default. Use elevation only to communicate interaction or hierarchy; avoid nested cards and decorative glass effects.

## Concept rules

Define one recognizable composition, one brand-connected typographic idea, one image-treatment rule, one deliberate color evolution, one verified emotional promise, and one conversion path.

## Quality bar

Raise the visual bar above category convention. The build must have a prospect-specific compositional thesis, three distinct art-directed moments, a deliberate typographic pairing, an image-treatment rule, and a mobile-native rhythm. Reject hero-plus-card-grid defaults, repeated generic cards, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, and familiar SaaS/agency templates unless evidence and concept require them. Every major section must earn its space through brand, proof, or conversion value.

## Components

- Sticky identity and direct contact navigation
- Problem-led intervention line
- Technical principle statement with founder attribution
- Direct meeting and budget email actions
- Compact address and contact footer

## Image treatment

Learning-space photography is cropped as architectural evidence rather than presented as a portfolio claim. Images receive one consistent restrained green treatment and factual descriptive alt text; no image is labeled as a Curiosità project.

## Mobile rhythm

The desktop project line becomes a single native vertical route with generous touch targets, compressed metadata, and alternating density between questions, answers, and calls to action. Core information and actions remain available without hover.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do tailor the system to the verified rebuild opportunity. Don't copy proprietary logos/photos/layouts/substantial text, invent claims, fabricate proof, or use a generic AI landing-page template.

This is an early visual direction. It must not be described as production-complete, approved, or ready to publish.
