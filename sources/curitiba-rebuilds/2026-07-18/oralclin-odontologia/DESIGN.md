# Design System: Evidence-bounded redesign seed

## Overview

Creative North Star: This concept can only belong to Oralclin because it turns the clinic's published promise of a healthy smile, attentive care, modern equipment, child-friendly experience and patient testimony into a quiet appointment journey rather than a generic dental-service grid.

The implemented composition is a continuous care path: reassurance, treatments, official space imagery, concise review proof, then visit/contact. It avoids a generic service-card grid and makes scheduling the only emphasized action.

## Colors

- Retained Oralclin blue: `#0B6E8A`
- Retained clinic teal: `#4AA6A0`
- Teal-tinted clinical surface: `#F1F9F7`
- Deep closing surface: `#0F1D1F`
- Appointment-only coral: `#B63325`

The page evolves from a bright clinical surface to a teal treatment pause and a deep contact conclusion. Coral is reserved for scheduling.

## Typography

Self-hosted Figtree carries navigation, clinical facts, treatments, reviews, and contact. Self-hosted Source Serif 4 is restricted to reassurance and major patient-facing headings. Neither is claimed as an official Oralclin font.

## Elevation

Flat by default. Hierarchy comes from surface changes, rules, type, and image crops; there are no decorative shadows, gradients, or glass cards.

## Concept rules

- Composition: a connected care path, not a service grid.
- Type: soft reassurance serif against factual high-legibility sans.
- Images: official clinic imagery only, cropped as fixed-ratio windows with no flattening overlay.
- Color: clinical light → teal pause → deep visit panel.
- Promise: attentive, calm care with modern equipment, including children and older patients.
- Conversion: one Portuguese scheduling route to the verified WhatsApp number, supported by phone and email.

## Quality bar

Raise the visual bar above category convention. The build must have a prospect-specific compositional thesis, three distinct art-directed moments, a deliberate typographic pairing, an image-treatment rule, and a mobile-native rhythm. Reject hero-plus-card-grid defaults, repeated generic cards, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, and familiar SaaS/agency templates unless evidence and concept require them. Every major section must earn its space through brand, proof, or conversion value.

## Components

Sticky navigation, hero image window, care-path statements, treatment rows, asymmetric space composition, score proof, resolved visit/contact panel, mobile disclosure navigation, and a separate independent proposal document.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do preserve verified identity cues and source every factual claim. Do keep contact visible and local-language. Do not invent procedures, outcomes, credentials, prices, hours, or testimonials. Do not link the production homepage to the proposal.
