# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Tutors in Curitiba looking for a veterinary clinic, especially people needing immediate orientation for a possible emergency, alongside people looking for consultations, specialist care, bathing and grooming, or 24-hour hospitalization.

## Product Purpose

Make the clinic's documented services, location, and contact paths easier to understand at the moment a tutor needs help. The primary success condition is that an emergency visitor can find a clear next action without scanning a generic contact journey.

## Brand Personality

Calm, direct, reassuring. The interface should reduce cognitive load in an urgent moment without inventing clinical promises.

## Anti-references

No bath-and-grooming-first hero, oversized blank zones, low-contrast testimonials, stale article-led home, or generic contact-only journey.

## Design Principles

- Put emergency orientation before elective services.
- Separate each need into a clear path.
- Show only sourced contact information and label ambiguity instead of guessing.
- Keep the address and route action easy to find.
- Use calm visual rhythm so the page remains usable under stress.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

High-contrast text, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, keyboard-accessible contact dialog, reduced-motion fallback, and responsive layouts for narrow screens.
