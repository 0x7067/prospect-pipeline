# Design

## Direction
Mineral teal and rusted coral on a true near-white surface: a calm, local business presence with a measured editorial rhythm, not a generic finance template.

## Tokens
- Primary: oklch(0.48 0.105 171)
- Deep ink: oklch(0.29 0.07 175)
- Accent: oklch(0.67 0.15 38)
- Background: oklch(0.985 0.006 175)
- Body ink: oklch(0.18 0.035 178)

## Typography
Trebuchet MS / Segoe UI system stack. Strong weight contrast, fluid display type, generous line-height in body copy.

## Layout
Asymmetric hero with a typographic lead and abstract local composition; a single varied list for service journeys; split process section; direct contact block. 4pt spacing scale with generous section separation.

## Interaction
Simple anchor navigation, mobile menu, clear external contact links, reduced-motion support.
