## Brand-source brief

- Logo variants: compact horizontal `logo2a.png`; stacked/architectural `logoa.png`.
- Retained colors: source blue `#1A59A8` (published inline in the official HTML); near-black logo ink approximated as `#211D1A`; white `#FFFFFF`.
- Color evolution: deep navy `#112C49` gives the official blue more editorial depth; pale mineral `#E8EDF2` and warm paper `#F2F0E9` create readable section contrast.
- Typography: current site loads Open Sans; the build uses system-safe Arial/Helvetica for the same direct sans-serif voice, paired with restrained Georgia italics to echo the script wordmark without imitating it.
- Current personality: established, direct, service-oriented.
- Strongest published language: “Mais que um contador: um escritório de contabilidade que entende o seu negócio”; “segurança e precisão”; “atendimento focado em excelência”.
- Verified location/contact: Rua Newton França Bitencourt, 460, Ahú, Curitiba–PR; 41 3018 7461; 41 98841 3809; atendimento@contabilidadecuritiba.com.
- Verified services: legalização de empresas/societário, fiscal, pessoal/trabalhista, contábil, IRPF; homepage also mentions contabilidade condominial.
- Proof boundary: 30+ years and CRC-PR registration are organization statements on the official homepage. No registration number, named professional, client, testimonial or performance result was found and none is invented.
- Brand equity preserved: architectural roof/desk mark, script signature, blue, 30+ years, local Ahú presence, precise/close-service language, official photography.
- Weaknesses not copied: oversized empty areas, unlabeled fields, blank H1, mobile overflow, low-resolution imagery used large, inconsistent hierarchy and foreign sitemap reference.

## Concept thesis

This concept can only belong to **Contabilidade Curitiba** because its roof/desk mark becomes an editorial “work surface”: a disciplined ledger rhythm organizes 30+ years of local, relationship-led service into clear next steps.

- Compositional idea: horizontal ledger rules and numbered entries, balanced by the roof-like curved brand gesture.
- Typographic idea: direct sans-serif structure with signature-like italic pivots.
- Image rule: official hero stays panoramic; low-resolution detail images remain a cropped, small-scale strip.
- Emotional promise: precise orientation and security, bounded by the office’s published mission and service language.
- Conversion path: identify a service → see the approach → contact directly by WhatsApp, phone or e-mail.
